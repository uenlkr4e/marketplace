# Parcelback: merchant outreach and interviews

**Goal:** find out whether Wix store owners have a real returns problem and would pay to solve it, before building more.

**Go / no-go (after 10–15 conversations):**
- **Go** if at least 3 merchants agree to try the beta and at least half handle 10+ returns a month.
- **No-go** if most handle returns rarely or are happy with email. Then revisit the Tiendanube SEO idea from the gap analysis.

## 1. Where to find Wix store owners

| Channel | How | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| r/WIX, r/smallbusiness, r/ecommerce | Comment helpfully on returns/shipping questions; post the "feedback" message below where self-promotion is allowed | Read each subreddit's rules; most ban direct promotion |
| Wix Studio community forum (forum.wixstudio.com) | Answer returns and order questions; mention the beta only when relevant | No spam; be genuinely helpful first |
| Facebook groups ("Wix website owners", "Wix eCommerce", niche store groups) | Post the feedback message; DM people who comment | Ask admins first in strict groups |
| Wix Partners and agencies (wix.com/marketplace) | Email agencies that build stores; they can bring several clients at once | Offer them something too: free for their clients during beta |
| Live Wix stores | Find stores via "Powered by Wix" + a niche (clothing, shoes, gifts), use their contact page | Keep it short and personal; at most one follow-up |

Track every contact in a simple sheet: **name, store, channel, date, status (contacted / replied / call booked / beta), returns per month, current process, would pay (y/n, amount), notes.**

## 2. Messages

### Community post (feedback request)

> **Title:** How do you handle returns on your Wix store?
>
> I'm building a small returns app for Wix stores, and I'd like to learn from people who actually run one before going further.
>
> Today Wix doesn't have a customer-facing returns page: customers email you, and you refund by hand. The app adds:
> - an order lookup page where customers track their order (no account needed),
> - a "Start a return" form with your return window and reasons,
> - a returns queue where you approve, reject (with a reason the customer sees) or refund in one click,
> - automatic emails to the customer at each step.
>
> How do you handle returns today, and roughly how many do you get a month? If you'd like to try it free during the beta, comment or DM me.

## 3. Interview script (15 minutes)

Listen more than you talk. Ask about **what they do today**, not what they'd like in theory.

**Their store (2 min)**
1. What do you sell, and roughly how many orders a month?
2. Where are most of your customers (country/EU)?

**Returns today (6 min)**
3. Walk me through the last return you handled, from the first message to the refund.
4. How many returns or exchanges do you get in a typical month?
5. What's the most annoying part? Where does time go?
6. Have customers asked "where's my order?" How do you answer them?
7. Have you tried any app or tool for returns? Why did you stop, or why not?
8. (EU sellers) Have you heard about the EU "withdrawal button" requirement from June 2026? How are you handling it?

**Demo (4 min)**: see section 4.

**Value and price (3 min)**
9. What would this replace for you? How much time would it save each month?
10. If this cost $10–25 a month, would you use it? What would make it worth more, or less?
11. Would you try the beta on your store in the next two weeks? (Note the exact answer.)

**After the call:** fill in the tracking sheet the same day, and quote their words exactly where you can.

## 4. Demo script (2–4 minutes)

Use the dev site (main dev site, order #10003/#10004 style test data).

1. **Customer view:** open the order lookup, enter order number + email, show items and tracking, click "Start a return", pick an item and reason, submit, show the return number.
2. **Merchant view:** open Returns in the dashboard, show the request, reject one with a reason, approve another, and show the refund window (manual vs automatic payments).
3. **Emails:** show the three emails in your inbox (received, approved, rejected with reason).
4. **Close:** "What would you change before using this on your store?"

## 5. What to watch for

- **Strong signals:** they describe a painful current process unprompted, have 10+ returns a month, already tried tools, or agree to a specific beta date.
- **Weak signals:** "sounds nice", "maybe later", or very few returns.
- **Feature requests to note:** exchanges, prepaid return labels, store credit, multi-language, live carrier tracking. Count how often each comes up before building any of them.
