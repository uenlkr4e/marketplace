Privacy policy

Parcelback is an app for stores built on Wix. It lets a store’s customers track orders, request returns and withdraw from a purchase, and lets the store handle those requests. This page explains what data that involves. We keep it to what the app needs to work.

Who we are

Parcelback is run by Shane Ulker, Selcuk Mah. Haci Bekir Sk. Gold Garden Sitesi 3. Giris No 23, Selcuklu/Konya, Turkey (“we”). For anything about privacy, email privacy@parcelback.app.

Two kinds of data, two roles

  • A store’s customers’ data (the people who buy from a store using Parcelback). The store is responsible for this data (the “controller”). We process it only on the store’s behalf, to provide the app, and never for our own purposes.
  • Store owners’ data (the businesses that install Parcelback). For this we are responsible ourselves.

If you bought from a store and have a question about your data, the quickest route is the store itself. We’ll help the store answer.

What we read but don’t keep

When a customer looks up an order, the app reads the order, its shipments and tracking links from the store’s Wix account to show them, and only if both the order number and the email match. When a store refunds, the app asks Wix which payments can be refunded. None of this is copied into our database. We never see card or bank details; payments stay with the store’s payment provider.

Abuse protection

To stop people guessing order numbers, we count requests per visitor. The counters are stored as one-way hashes, so they contain no IP address or other readable identifier, and old counters are deleted after about a day.

Emails

Emails about a request (received, approved, rejected, withdrawal confirmation) are sent by Wix from the store, through the store’s Wix Automations, under Wix’s privacy policy.

Cookies

Parcelback itself sets no cookies and runs no tracking or analytics, neither in the store nor on this website. Wix may set its own cookies on the store’s site.

Data about store owners

  • The store’s Wix app instance ID, which identifies the store to us.
  • The store’s Parcelback settings: return window, return reasons, final-sale products (their names and IDs).
  • Anything you send us when you contact support.

We use this to provide the app and to answer support requests (legal basis: performing our contract with you). Billing, when paid plans start, is handled by Wix; we don’t receive payment details.

We don’t sell data and don’t share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Return and withdrawal requests: while the store has Parcelback installed, so the store has a record of them. A store can ask us to delete individual requests at any time.
  • After a store uninstalls Parcelback: we delete the store’s requests and settings within 30 days. Export them first (Returns → Export CSV) if you need them.
  • Abuse-protection counters: about one day.
  • Support emails: up to two years after the conversation ends.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. If you’re a store’s customer, contact the store first; we’ll help them. If you’re a store owner, email privacy@parcelback.app. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we’ll tell store owners before they take effect.
