Setup

Where does the “Track your order” page appear?

Parcelback adds an order lookup widget to your site when you install it. In the Editor you can move it to any page; a dedicated page linked from your menu or footer works best. Select the widget and open Settings to change its heading or language.

Do customers need an account?

No. They enter their order number and the email they used at checkout. Both must match before anything is shown.

How do I set my return policy?

In your dashboard go to Parcelback → Settings. There you can switch returns on or off, set the return window (counted from the first shipment), choose return reasons and mark final-sale products.

Returns & refunds

What happens when a customer requests a return?

The request appears under Parcelback → Returns and the customer gets a confirmation email. Approve it (the customer is told to send the items back) or reject it with a reason, which is included in the email to the customer. Once the items are back, mark them as received and refund.

Why does the refund say “record refund” instead of “refund”?

Some payment methods, such as bank transfer or cash on delivery, can’t be refunded automatically through Wix. Send the money to the customer yourself; Parcelback records the refund in Wix so your order stays correct.

Can I put returned items back in stock?

Yes. The refund window has a “put the returned items back in stock” option, on by default.

EU withdrawal

How do I turn on the withdrawal button?

In the Editor, select the order lookup widget, open Settings and switch on EU withdrawal button. It’s off by default, so stores outside the EU aren’t affected.

What does the customer get?

A confirmation screen with the date and time, a reference number and the items, plus a confirmation email with the same details. The withdrawal then appears in your Returns list with a “refund due by” date.

Is this legal advice?

No. It’s a tool to help you comply. Check your obligations, exempt products and policy wording with a lawyer if you’re unsure.

Emails & languages

Who sends the customer emails, and can I change them?

Wix sends them from your store through Wix Automations, and they count toward your site’s email quota. Edit the wording, sender and design under Automations in your dashboard.

Which languages are supported?

The customer page is available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese. It follows your site’s language automatically; you can also pick one in the widget’s Settings. The dashboard and the default emails are in English for now.

How do I export my requests?

Go to Parcelback → Returns and click Export CSV. The file includes every request and its full status history.
