04

The EU withdrawal button, done

Since June 2026, online stores selling to consumers in the EU must offer a clearly labelled way to withdraw from a contract online. Parcelback adds it to your order page with one switch.

  • “Withdraw from contract here” and “Confirm withdrawal here” buttons
  • No reason asked, everything preselected
  • Timestamped confirmation on screen and by email
  • Withdrawals in your returns queue, with refund due dates and a CSV export

A tool to help you comply, not legal advice.
